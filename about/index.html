About Tibyan

Tibyan is two engineers. Abrar works on machine learning and LLM systems from Riyadh. Between us we take a project from the scope call to a running system.

Why “Tibyan”

Tibyān (تبيان) is an Arabic word for making something thoroughly clear. It is found in the Quran, in An-Nahl 16:89. We chose it because clarity is the standard we want to be held to: a clear scope, a clear baseline, and a clear account of what the system gets wrong.

How we work

  • We measure the starting point before we build.
  • We publish the evaluation with the system.
  • We say what is out of scope, in writing, before work begins.
  • We make no claim on this site that we could not show you evidence for.
