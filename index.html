How an engagement runs

  1. Scope call. Thirty minutes, free. You describe the problem and the data. If we are the wrong people for it, we say so on the call.
  2. Baseline. We measure how your current method performs, even when that method is a spreadsheet. That number becomes the target.
  3. Pilot. Two to four weeks at a fixed price. One system, measured against the baseline, delivered with a written error report.
  4. Handover. Code, documentation and a runbook, in your repository.
