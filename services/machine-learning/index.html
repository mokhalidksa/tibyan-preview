Book a call

Forecasting and risk scoring, measured against what you use today

We build models that predict a number or score a risk from your operational data. Every project begins by measuring your current method, so you know what the model has to beat and whether it did.

Problems we take on

  • Demand, volume and delay forecasting for operations planning
  • Fraud and risk scoring where a decision must be made in milliseconds
  • Classification and ranking on tabular business data
  • Rebuilding a slow or unreliable training pipeline
  • An independent evaluation of a model someone else built

How we work

We agree one metric with you before any modelling. We backtest a simple baseline first, because a baseline often turns out to be harder to beat than expected. The model is then tested on data it has not seen, over several time windows, and you receive the error for each window.

A typical pilot

Three weeks. Week one covers data access and the baseline. Week two covers the model and backtests. Week three covers the error report, a walkthrough call and the handover.

What we do not do

We do not promise an accuracy figure before seeing data. We do not build models for decisions about individuals that you could not explain to those individuals.

Questions

How much data do we need? For forecasting, at least two full seasonal cycles of history is a sensible minimum. We check this on the scope call.

Where does the model run? In your cloud account or on your servers. We hand over the code and the deployment steps.

What if the model does not beat the baseline? Then the error report says so, and you have spent a pilot fee to learn that the simple method is enough.
